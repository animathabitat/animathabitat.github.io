In Memoriam

                    
Dr Dame Valerie Jane Morris Goodall ( 1934-2025 )  ·  Founder of the Fondateur du Jane Goodall Institute

                    
Remembered as a conservationist, ethologist and primatologist, in particular, for early fieldwork studying chimpanzees at Gombe, in Tanganyika. That study continues to this day in Tanzania. It is the longest continuous study of any animal in a natural habitat.
